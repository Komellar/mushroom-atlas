## Items requiring particular attention

- Conditional edibility: Amanita rubescens, Armillaria mellea, and Kuehneromyces mutabilis. Raw toxicity, individual adverse reactions, and dangerous lookalikes are explicit in notes; a category badge cannot establish food safety.
- Conflicting edibility assessments: Chlorophyllum rhacodes is marked poisonous with a qualification explaining source disagreement. Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca is treated as inedible under the First Nature recommendation. Confirm the proposed terminology for a Polish audience.
- Taxonomic groups: Leccinum, Russula, Armillaria, and Chlorophyllum can require microscopy. Source labels and visual resemblance are preliminary evidence, not a diagnostic conclusion.
- Galerina marginata versus Kuehneromyces mutabilis: neither substrate nor visible stem texture gives a guaranteed distinction. The comparison note says microscopy may be required.
- Occurrence ranges for expanded entries are approximate temperate-European source ranges, not a measured Polish regional calendar. Local timing, winter exceptions, and tree associations need review.
- Initial source records use accepted synonyms where file labels use older names, including Boletus badius and Macrolepiota rhacodes. Confirm every mapping and avoid extending a synonym to a lookalike.
- Some sources have inconsistent detail: First Nature and public Polish guidance differ on milk-colour progression in Lactarius deliciosus and drying patterns in Kuehneromyces. The atlas omits absolute claims about those disputed progressions. Older guides also contain edibility claims that should not be generalized to species outside this dataset.
- Some photograph sets show the same specimen in different views. Some omit an exposed stem base or cross-section; captions identify visible limitations. Request additional photographs if the set does not adequately illustrate the species.
